How to Export from Power BI

  1. Open your Power BI report
  2. Click on a table or visual
  3. Click ... (More options)
  4. Select Export data
  5. Choose CSV format
  6. Open the file & copy contents here

Or just select cells in a table and Ctrl+C to copy!
